The surrogate replaces a simulator call of 8.94\,s (median,
single operating point) with a forward pass of
2.4\,ms for a single point and
0.25\,\textmu s per point when batched on one GPU ---
a measured speed-up of $\approx 3.6
\times 10^{7}$, which turns exhaustive sweeps of the
operating envelope from a cluster-scale computation into an interactive one.
